## What I'm Exploring This Summer

Most businesses struggle not because the idea is bad — but because they're built without really understanding people. No real planning, no audience research, ignoring how the world is shifting digitally.

I want to understand the full picture:

- How do real people find and use online tools?
- How does DNS, hosting, and domains work together to get something live?
- What actually makes someone click, stay, or leave?

## How I Think It Should Work

Talk to real people first. Understand what frustrates them, what excites them, what they actually need — then design something that fits. Demo it. Test it. Refine it.

Simple in theory. Hard in practice. That's what I'm here to figure out.
